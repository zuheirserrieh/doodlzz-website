# Doodlzz — Project Brief (handoff from claude.ai)

Doodlzz is a baby & kids products store in Lebanon. Goal: an e-commerce website, mobile-first.
Reference site for the idea (not to copy): https://toys4less.com

## Status
- Mobile homepage + menu drawer designed and approved for review.
- Design mockups: `Main.dc.html` (homepage), `Menu.dc.html` (menu drawer) — keep copies in `docs/design/`.
- **Platform decided: custom Next.js site** (this repo). Orders go through WhatsApp for now.
- Next: real content from the owner → payments/accounts → product management (headless Shopify or a CMS).

## Homepage structure (from the owner, in order)
1. Nav bar: menu, logo, settings, sign in, shopping cart (with item count)
2. Search bar + Shop by age (0–6m, 6–12m, 1–2y, 2–4y, 4+ — to be confirmed with owner's image)
3. Hero slideshow or video
4. Catalog (category grid)
5. Floating WhatsApp button (side, always visible)
6. Best sellers & New arrivals (tabs)
7. "Real Moments with Doodlzz" — slideshow of customer review photos
8. Brands we carry — auto-scrolling logo strip
9. Follow us: Instagram / Facebook / TikTok
10. About us + footer (Shop links, Help links)

## Visual style
- Soft, playful baby-store look; pastel category tiles; rounded corners (12–24px); pill buttons.
- Fonts: Fredoka (headings, logo), Nunito (body) — Google Fonts. Arabic: Tajawal.
- Colors:
  - Navy text / dark UI: #1E2742
  - Accent (buttons, cart badge, "zz" in logo): #C8462A (coral) — adjustable
  - Pastels: blue #E6F1FA · peach #FCE7DE · yellow #FFF2C7 · mint #E2F3EA · lilac #EDE7F8
  - Light grey surfaces: #F3F5F8 · footer cream: #F6F3EE
  - WhatsApp green: #158F45 · badge yellow: #FFE08A
- Top strip rotates: "Delivery available all over Lebanon 🇱🇧" / "Pay cash on delivery or by Whish Money".
